A tradition of careful stewardship

Greenwood Estate Care was founded on a simple belief: that a well-kept landscape speaks before you ever reach the front door. We serve homeowners who value discretion, consistency, and results you can see from the curb — and enjoy from the terrace.

From weekly lawn maintenance to full garden design, our crews arrive on schedule, leave no trace, and treat your property as though it were our own.

Our services

Comprehensive care for lawns, gardens, and outdoor living spaces.

Lawn Maintenance

Weekly mowing, edging, fertilization, and aeration programs tailored to your turf. Striped finishes and weed-free beds, season after season.

Landscape Design

Custom planting plans, hardscape integration, and garden layouts that complement your home's architecture and mature gracefully over time.

Seasonal Planting

Spring bulbs, summer annuals, autumn chrysanthemums, and winter evergreen arrangements — refreshed on a schedule you never need to think about.

Estate Maintenance

Hedge trimming, tree and shrub care, irrigation management, and fall leaf removal for larger properties and country estates.
